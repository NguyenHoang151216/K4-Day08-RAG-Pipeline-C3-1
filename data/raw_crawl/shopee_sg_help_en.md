## Hi, how can we help?

You can check the status of your orders via My Purchases in the Me tab on the Shopee App. All of your orders will be categorized according to their order statuses: To Pay - comprises of all unpaid orders To Ship - comprises all paid orders that have not been shipped by sellers To Receive - comprises of all orders that have been shipped Completed - comprises all orders that have been delivered Canceled - comprises all orders that have been canceled Return/Refund - comprises all orders that have been returned or refunded Tracking the delivery status of orders When your order is in the To Ship or To Receive status, you can view detailed shipping updates and track its delivery status more closely. Orders shipped by Shopee Supported Logistics will have tracking numbers and can be easily tracked in detail via the Shopee App. If your order does not have a tracking number, you may contact the seller directly to check your order’s delivery status.

## Hi, how can we help?

To track your overseas order, Go to To Ship or To Receive under My Purchases in the Shopee App > Select the shipment status of your order > View all shipping updates. Please be advised that the tracking status of your order may take between 24 to 48 hours to be updated by our logistics partners. If you require immediate tracking of your order, we recommend that you directly contact the seller through Shopee Chat. Starting from July 12th, 2022, the Standard Economy option will no longer be available for overseas orders to ensure better trackability. For orders placed before July 12th, 2022 using the Standard Economy option, they will not be trackable and may be delivered to your letterbox via SingPost Normal Mail.

## Hi, how can we help?

If you have not received your order by the Estimated Delivery Date, there are three actions you can take: To submit a return/refund request, go to Me tab on Shopee App > Select To Receive > Select Order > Select Return/Refund >  Select Reason as I didn’t receive my items > Select Parcel not delivered > Select Confirm > Provide other necessary details > Select Submit . Once your request has been submitted, Shopee will conduct an investigation and provide a response. You can check the shipment status of your order directly with the logistics partner . For orders shipped using non-Shopee Supported Logistics , you may contact the seller directly instead. If the seller is unresponsive, you can raise a refund request.
